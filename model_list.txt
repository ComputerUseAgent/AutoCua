AutoCua - Supported Providers & Models
========================================

In main.py, pass them straight to run_agent:

    run_agent(
        mode="computer use",
        provider="<provider-name>",
        model="<model-name>",
        task="...",
    )

Copy the names exactly as written below (case-sensitive).
`thinking` is the reasoning level the agents send; `coder` is the level
Coder mode sends instead (set per model as coder_effort in each
provider's view.py, next to the normal one).
A name that is not on this list is NOT validated - it is sent to the
provider verbatim and comes back as a 404 model_not_found.
